The day has arrived!! And it's AWESOME! Now I know I promised a video of the [ahem] "pin extraction," but I want to save that since it's long, and I need to find the perfect theme song to set it to, and it's end of day. So it'll come tomorrow!

But I have other fun stuff. First: WEIGHT BEARING STARTS BACK! That's right, we are now at Stage: partial weight-bearing (PWB)! Whoo hoo!! It'll be a few weeks of continuing to use the crutches, but I'm allowed to put my foot down and *dare I say*... walk on it.

<img src="/entries/bothfeet.png" alt="A side-by-side of two lateral foot x-rays. Right, labeled 'RIGHT FOOT 6 WEEKS, POST OP', shows smaller, tidier hardware and a heel that already looks solidly knit together." class="float-left" />The reason I can do this is because the x-rays came back and showed significant bone regrowth at just the 6-week mark (!!). As you can see in the photo, at 6 weeks last time, my heel hadn't even begun to fuse. This go-round, it's like I'd already been walking on it for a couple of weeks. This is *huge*, because it means that even as I've aged 4 years, my body has done better this time at healing itself. Wonder why that is... < cough-cough, peptides >. Anyway, the doctor approved and said to ease into the full crutch-free life.

There were some other x-rays showing how likely I'll be to set off metal detectors in the coming months and years (spoiler: VERY).

I had 5 cuts: two in the heel, two in the big toe to fix the bunion, and one on the second toe to get rid of the necrosis. My second toe will no longer bend at that second joint, but I'm told I'll still be able to run and exercise. The toe *will adapt*. Finally, I wanted to show these because you can also see the pins in the toe, a final homage to my shish-k-toe right there in B&W. It's been real, it's been fun, but it hasn't been real fun, so getting that out was simply amazing. More on that tomorrow, but for this evening, me and my toe are hanging out and enjoying the pin-free life.

One sort-of-crutch-free step at a time.
