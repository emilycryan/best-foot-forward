I have fallen behind on writing here, and I could not be happier about the reason: I have been too busy being *active*. Things are going SO WELL. I have been strapping on my RecoverX brace and taking the pups to the park a couple of times a day, my two-and-a-half-month check-in is DONE, and I have the x-rays to prove how amazing it all looks. Right now, life is surprisingly, wonderfully good.

It is crazy to think that three months ago I was hobbling around in severe pain, watching every small footfall, trying to hold an impossibly hard position that was impossible precisely because a whole inner ankle tendon was no longer doing its job. Back then, I did not imagine that jumping forward three months would land me somewhere *less* painful than that exact moment in time. But here we are.

So here is the moral of the story: do not waste life and time when things hurt. Take care of them before they suck up one more moment of what is left of the good years. Perhaps by this time next year, I will be gearing up for another long(ish) race.

Anyway, doctor's orders: keep up PT for another three months, and stay active with things like walking, yoga, biking (NOPE) and/or the elliptical (DOUBLE NOPE). I get to restart my [\[solidcore\]](https://www.solidcore.co/) membership in two weeks, along with my deep core work, and hopefully some balance work and lower body classes too.
